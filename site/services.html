Services

Hands-on security work. Separate from the platform.

These engagements stand on their own. Our engineers do the assessments, the red teaming, the response drills and the hardening whether or not you ever licence the platform. Where our own tooling makes the work faster we use it, but none of it is a prerequisite. Platform onboarding and ongoing product support are included in the annual subscription. These broader engagements are scoped separately.

01

AI security

MCPAI stackAI agentsagentic apps

Security review of the systems you are building on AI: MCP servers and the tools they expose, the AI stack underneath, AI agents, and agentic applications. We test what an agent can reach, what a hostile prompt can make it do, and what shows up in your logs when it does.

02

Cloud security assessment and review

postureidentitylogging

A structured review of your cloud posture, identity, logging coverage and blast radius, benchmarked against how named adversaries actually operate rather than a generic checklist.

03

Red teaming assessment

red teamfull chainfindings report

A red teaming assessment of your environment. Our engineers attack it end to end, the way a named APT would, and a findings report maps every step to what your detections did and did not catch.

04

Cloud security training

workshophands-onteam upskilling

Hands-on sessions on the real cloud attack surface, identity and the data services, taught through live emulations instead of slides. Your engineers learn how attackers actually move, by watching it happen.

05

Incident response training

live-firetabletopblue team

Tabletop and live-fire IR drills against cloud breach scenarios. Your responders work real telemetry from a cloud breach as it unfolds, so the practice looks exactly like the day it matters.

06

CI/CD security

pipelinesupply chainsecrets

Secure the path from commit to deploy: pipeline identity, build-time secrets, supply-chain integrity and artifact signing, so the way you ship code is not the way you get breached.

07

Infrastructure security

Harden the cloud foundation: network design, infrastructure-as-code and account guardrails, so the gaps we find stay closed and do not quietly reopen on the next deploy.
