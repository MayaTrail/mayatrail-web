MayaTrail website, rework preview
=================================
Built 2026-10-16 from mayatrail-web, branch feat/website-rework.

HOW TO LOOK AT IT
-----------------
Unzip, then double-click index.html. Any modern browser will do.

Chrome or Edge is worth using for this one. Two effects only they
support yet: each surface fades up as you scroll it into view, and the
hop to a blog post crossfades instead of blinking. Safari and Firefox
show the same site without those two things, which is the intended
fallback, not a bug.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR
----------------
The site is ONE SCROLL. Platform, Services, Pricing and Field notes are
four surfaces in a single page. Scroll off the end of one and you carry
straight into the next; you should never need the tabs at the top. Watch
the nav mark itself as each surface passes under it.

On the platform surface, the bordered panel headed "Every detection ends
in one of three places" advances on its own. Hover it, or tab into it,
and it holds.

Field notes sits at the foot of the scroll with the latest posts on it.
A whole post is still its own page, so it can be linked and indexed on
its own.
